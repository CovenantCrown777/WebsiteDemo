Who is Simone L. Blackmon?

Hello! I am Simone Lytesha Blackmon, a Zion, IL native, mother, middle school educator, journalist, and hairstylist. My mission is to merge education, social-emotional learning (SEL), and haircare through web development.

I'm interested in building digital tools that focus on identity, confidence, and developmental intelligence. I passionately aim to create impactful staples that purposely serve cultural diversity and structured interpersonal growth.

I love to explore foods from different cultures, I also love to visit new places. Currently, The Garden Brunch Cafe and Taconganas of Memphis have my heart! I also love iced coffee from 7Brew and any dish that contains protein, tomatoes, lettuce, sour cream and queso!Yum!
